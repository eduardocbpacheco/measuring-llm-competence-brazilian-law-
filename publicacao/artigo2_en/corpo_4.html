5Discussion

The three measurements point the same way by independent routes: the legal competence of a language model is not a single quantity, and the convention of reporting it as one number per model and per task erases exactly the differences that matter to whoever will use the result. Writing the correct document and judging whether someone else's document is correct are competences associated at ρ = 0.41 on the part that decides the score. Getting the addressing right and getting the thesis right are measures that order differently and have different leaders. And the same judge, on the same task, agrees with the human very differently depending on the area of law, with a median range of 0.279 within one of the top nine, against 0.047 of distance among those same nine.

The most direct practical consequence concerns the choice of judge. The question "which is the best judge" is ill-posed before the answer is measured, because aggregate agreement is a weighted mean that reflects above all the most numerous subtask: here, the substance class has 413 of the 561 criteria and determines 98% of the ordering. The answerable question is which is the best judge for the subtask and the area that matter, and it has different answers. When an external decomposition is available, and the grading guidelines of an examining board are a ready-made decomposition the researcher did not invent, decomposing before choosing costs little and changes the choice.

The second consequence concerns what can be published as an ordering. In the data here, the order at the top is not informative: nine judges are within each other's noise on document substance, and the order among them changes from run to run. What we propose is not to abandon the table, but to accompany it with the noise floor and to mark which positions are distinguishable. It is cheap: it requires repeating the protocol, which careful work already does, and converting the resulting dispersion into a threshold, which almost nobody does. Reproducibility has at least three layers and the field reports one. The first is the seed, the only one reported regularly, and fixing it does not solve the problem. The second is batch size: with a fixed seed and temperature zero, the output still depends on what it was processed alongside. This is not a defect, it is floating-point arithmetic, and it has no trivial fix, but it is reportable. The third is the identity of the model, and it is the most serious, because no discipline of code resolves it: the object measured sits on the other side of an application programming interface, and the same name two months later is another model or another implementation, with no way to know which. A published κ value is verifiable within a window of time and an inference configuration that nobody describes in full.

6Limitations

The gold standard has three annotators and was not extended: the 940 annotations are inherited from Rabula [5], and the ceiling of the experiment is the same as the original one, the agreement among the three annotators, at 0.789 and 0.763. The benchmark has 105 questions and seven areas, which is little for the readings by area, where each cell rests on 15 questions; the extension to 314 questions is planned. The noise floor was measured in the best case: the value of 0.0101 measures only the variation under repeated inference with frozen code, and does not include variation from a change of prompt, of library or of model version, so the real floor across laboratories is necessarily higher and was not measured. For the same reason, the two-month drift was observed in one model and not measured systematically. All judges received a single formulation of the judging prompt, and it is possible that some respond better to another and that the order changes with it, which was not tested. Finally, the comparison among the correlations of the three panels of Figure 1 is limited by sample power, as Section 4.4 itself states.

7Conclusion

We measured 31 language models in three roles over the same legal material, the same 105 questions from the second stage of the OAB Bar Exam and the same 940 criteria of the official guidelines, with a gold standard from 3 legal experts, and we report the three roles by area of law and by class of criterion. Knowing how to write a legal document and knowing how to judge someone else's are associated competences, and the association weakens as the task stops demanding form and starts demanding substance, from ρ = 0.67 on the discursive question to 0.41 on the substantive criteria of the document. Document drafting is not a single task for measurement purposes: split into the classes the board labels, it produces two orderings that agree at ρ = 0.48 and have different leaders. And the agreement of a single judge varies across areas of law far more than the top judges vary among themselves, which makes a single number per judge an insufficient description of the ruler.

Three reporting recommendations follow, and none requires new data. Report the agreement table accompanied by the noise floor, marking which positions are distinguishable, because in ours the top nine are not. Decompose the task before choosing the judge whenever an external decomposition is available, because aggregate agreement reflects the most numerous subtask and not necessarily the one that matters. And treat batch size as part of the protocol, alongside temperature and seed, because it also determines the output. All of them require only reporting what has already been measured and is currently discarded.
